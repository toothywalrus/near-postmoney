Acme Robotics, Inc.

Its SAFEs raise USDC into escrow, become NFTs, and convert themselves at the next priced round by the post-money SAFE's own formula, to the share. Its board is a Sputnik DAO: every issuance is a proposal it votes on, against a hash of the numbers it saw.

§3

SAFE certificates

Each SAFE is a NEP-171 token minted to its investor when the board closes the round. The certificate is drawn by the contract itself. It moves only with the board's consent and is burned when it converts.

§4

If Acme is sold

The contract's waterfall view: each preferred holder takes its 1x preference or converts to common, whichever pays more; each SAFE its purchase back or its conversion amount.

§5

The Series A, as the chain executed it

Acme is the company in YC's Post-Money Safe User Guide, Appendix II, Example 1. Its board ran Question 2's Series A on testnet: $5m at $15m pre-money, a 1,695,000-share pool increase.
